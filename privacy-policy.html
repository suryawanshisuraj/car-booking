Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Reviewed: March 2026 • Commercial Fleet Standards

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

Vijay Tours and Travels ("we", "our", or "us") operates authorized commercial tourist taxi and intercity transport services across Maharashtra, including Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, Pune, and Shirdi. We are dedicated to respecting and safeguarding the personal privacy of all passengers, corporate clients, and website visitors.

This Privacy Policy explains the nature of personal information collected through our official website (vijaytours.com), direct telephone bookings, and WhatsApp dispatch channels, as well as how this information is used and protected.

2. Personal Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly required to schedule, confirm, and execute commercial passenger transport services:

  • Contact Details: Full name, telephone/mobile number, and WhatsApp contact handle.
  • Trip Information: Pickup address or landmark, destination city or address, scheduled travel date, and pickup time.
  • Transit Details: Flight numbers (for airport pickups to track delayed arrivals), passenger count, and luggage specifications.
  • Billing Details: Company name and GSTIN number when corporate tax invoicing is specifically requested.
Important: We do not store or process payment card numbers, CVVs, or online banking passwords on our servers. All digital payments are processed through verified third-party banking UPI applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or direct bank transfer).

3. Purpose of Data Processing

The information collected from passengers is utilized exclusively for operational transportation purposes:

  • Confirming vehicle availability and driver assignment.
  • Sending driver name, contact number, and commercial vehicle registration details prior to pickup.
  • Live routing and coordination between our central dispatch office and the assigned chauffeur.
  • Generating clear, itemized travel receipts including actual FASTag toll deductions.
  • Responding to customer service inquiries, lost item recovery, and trip modifications.

4. Strict Policy Against Selling or Renting Data

We uphold a zero-tolerance policy regarding data monetization. Vijay Tours and Travels does not sell, lease, rent, trade, or transfer passenger contact numbers or personal details to telemarketers, third-party advertising brokers, or unauthorized entities under any circumstances.

5. Chauffeur & Operational Coordination Disclosure

To facilitate timely pickup, your designated driver will receive your pickup landmark, destination, and contact phone number approximately 2 hours before scheduled departure. All chauffeurs in our fleet are bound by professional conduct guidelines and are prohibited from utilizing passenger contact data for purposes other than trip fulfillment.

6. Communication Protocols via WhatsApp and Phone

By submitting a booking request through our website or initiating contact via WhatsApp, you consent to receive direct transactional messages regarding your trip status, driver details, and billing summaries. We do not send automated spam, promotional newsletter blasts, or unrelated third-party offers.

7. Website Analytics and Cookies

Our website uses minimal, non-intrusive technical cookies and basic traffic metrics to ensure responsive page loading, form functionality, and secure server performance. We do not use invasive third-party cross-site trackers or behavioral profiling tools.

8. Data Retention & Passenger Rights

We retain booking logs and trip receipts only for the duration mandated by transport authorities, tax accounting regulations, and dispute resolution. You may request the verification, correction, or deletion of your personal records from our active dispatch database at any time by contacting our central control desk.
