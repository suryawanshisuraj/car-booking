Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Reviewed: March 2026 • Commercial Carriage Policy

1. Operational Framework & Agreement

These Terms & Conditions govern all transport bookings, outstation journeys, airport drops, and hourly vehicle rentals provided by Jay Bhavani Tours and Travels ("the Operator"). By confirming a booking online, via telephone, or through WhatsApp dispatch, the passenger ("Customer") agrees to these operational terms.

All vehicles in our fleet operate under All-India / All-Maharashtra Tourist Passenger Permits (AITP) with verified commercial registration (yellow number plates), commercial fitness certifications, and speed limiter compliance.

2. Booking Confirmation & Driver Allocation

Bookings submitted through the website are received directly by our central dispatch desk. A booking is formally confirmed once pickup details, route feasibility, and vehicle category are validated by our dispatch coordinator via WhatsApp or phone call.

The assigned chauffeur's name, mobile number, and vehicle registration number are transmitted to the customer approximately 2 hours prior to the scheduled departure time to ensure smooth, punctual boarding.

3. Fares, Kilometer Calculation & Toll Transparency

Our pricing is structured with complete commercial transparency and zero hidden surges:

  • Outstation Round Trips: Calculated based on a standard minimum billing of 300 km per calendar day. Kilometer distance is calculated garage-to-garage from our nearest operational hub.
  • Fixed Corridor Drops: Specific point-to-point routes (e.g., Mumbai to Pune, Navi Mumbai to Airport T2) feature all-inclusive fixed rates as quoted by the dispatch desk.
  • Highway Tolls & FASTag: Highway toll booth charges (Expressway, Samruddhi Mahamarg, Atal Setu / MTHL) are billed at actual cost. Digital FASTag transaction statements or printed toll receipts are provided to the passenger.
  • Parking & State Taxes: Airport entry/parking tickets and any municipal parking fees are payable by the customer at actuals.
Zero Surge Guarantee: Jay Bhavani Tours and Travels does not implement algorithmic price surge multipliers during peak hours, rainstorms, or festival periods. Quoted fares remain locked once confirmed by dispatch.

4. Driver Night Allowances & Operational Hours

For outstation journeys involving driving or vehicle retention between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM, a standard driver night allowance of ₹300 to ₹400 per night applies (depending on vehicle category). This allowance covers driver lodging and food subsistence during outstation layovers.

5. Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

We provide fair and flexible cancellation terms:

  • City & Airport Drops: Free cancellation up to 3 hours prior to scheduled pickup time. Cancellations requested less than 2 hours before pickup, or passenger no-shows, may incur a standard dispatch compensation fee of ₹400.
  • Outstation Round Trips: Free cancellation up to 6 hours prior to scheduled departure.
  • Flight Delays: For airport pickups, flight tracking is monitored when flight numbers are provided. No waiting charge is applied for flight delays up to 60 minutes after scheduled landing.

6. Luggage & Seating Capacity Compliance

In strict compliance with RTO commercial vehicle regulations, passengers must adhere to designated seating limits:

  • Sedan (Swift Dzire / Hyundai Aura): Maximum 4 passengers + 2 large luggage bags.
  • MPV (Maruti Ertiga): Maximum 6 passengers + 2-3 medium bags.
  • Executive SUV (Toyota Innova Crysta): Maximum 7 passengers + 4 large luggage bags.
  • Tempo Traveller: Maximum 13, 17, or 20 passengers strictly within vehicle seating permit.

Overloading of passengers or carriage of hazardous, inflammable, or contraband cargo is strictly prohibited.

7. Vehicle Cleanliness & Passenger Conduct

To maintain premium vehicle hygiene for all travelers, consumption of alcohol, smoking, and carriage of illegal substances inside the vehicle is strictly forbidden. Any vehicle damage or deep stains resulting from gross passenger negligence will incur actual professional sanitization costs.

8. Force Majeure & Route Disruptions

While every effort is made to maintain punctuality, the Operator shall not be liable for journey delays caused by extreme weather conditions, landslides on ghat corridors, unexpected highway roadblocks, or statutory traffic diversions. In the rare event of a mechanical breakdown, our central dispatch coordinates immediate roadside relief or a substitute vehicle without extra hire charge.

9. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These terms are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India and the Motor Vehicles Act applicable in the State of Maharashtra. Any disputes arising out of transport contracts shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Maharashtra.
